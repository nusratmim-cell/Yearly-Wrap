Part 1 · The product

What Direction B is

Three directions were prototyped (A Poster, B Marksheet, C Journey). B is the Marksheet: the year as a Shikho notebook (খাতা), a rubber-stamp for the learner type, and a marksheet share card at the end. It lives inside the real app home and opens from a banner. Tap the right half of the phone to advance, left half to go back, press-and-hold to pause.

Entry · on the real home screen

তোমার ২০২৬ খাতা — the banner that opens the story

The Wrap is not a separate app. It sits on the paid student’s home as a banner card — “তোমার ২০২৬ খাতা / সারা বছরের পড়ার হিসাব এক খাতায়” with a খাতা খোলা button. Tapping it opens the full-screen story.

Placement: prime home slot, above the lesson cards, under the greeting (হ্যালো, নিশাত).
Who sees it: paid students only, first release (SC-1737).
Build note: in the current app, this slot is occupied by the profile card + a 6-step collapsing hero — the banner must be designed to live inside that scroll behaviour design

Slide 1 · profile

The notebook cover — নাম · শ্রেণি · বিভাগ

The story opens on a “বার্ষিক খাতা” cover that writes the student’s identity in by hand: নাম নিশাত · শ্রেণি নবম · বিভাগ বিজ্ঞান. Pure personalisation, zero computation — it just makes the notebook theirs.

Data: Students.fist_name (sic), Students.class, Students.study_group have
Window: current profile.
Check: Students.class may not update yearly — BI (Mahizebin/Novera) to confirm it reflects the 2026 class.
মোট পড়ার সময়
Slide 2 · study time

মোট পড়ার সময় — ১৪২ ঘণ্টা

Total time studied, made concrete: ১৪২ ঘণ্টা ÷ ৬ = ২৩.৭ → প্রায় ২৪টা পুরো স্কুল দিনের সমান (each notebook mark = one 6-hour school day). Turning a raw number into something a 15-year-old feels.

Data: sum of all *_duration columns in user_activity_duration_agg (seconds) have
Framing: “school days” = total_seconds ÷ (6×3600). A presentation setting, not a new column.
Window: 2026-01-01 → cut-off date in December.
লাইভ ক্লাসের হাজিরা
Slide 3 · live-class attendance

লাইভ ক্লাসের হাজিরা — ৬৩ / ৭২

An attendance register (হাজিরা খাতা) that ticks off every class: তুমি ৭২টার মধ্যে ৬৩টা লাইভ ক্লাসে ছিলে. High attendance earns a title stamp — ফার্স্ট বেঞ্চার (এই বছরের খেতাব).

Joined: student_attendees, counting a class only when the student stayed ≥ a minimum (e.g. 10 min or 30% of class length) have
Held: lessons where content_type = LiveClass, by academic_program_id, from enrolment date.
Rate: joined ÷ held. Join on content_id (or room_id when empty) to teacher_attendees for class length.
Badge: “ফার্স্ট বেঞ্চার” = a tier on attendance rate (thresholds are settings, not code) tier config
কুইজের হিসাব
Slide 4 · quizzes

কুইজের হিসাব — ১৪৮টা কুইজ

Quizzes done across the year, drawn as a cumulative graph (প্রতি মাস শেষে মোট কুইজ, মার্চ → ডিসেম্বর), with a nudge — আর ২টা হলেই কুইজ চ্যাম্পিয়ন — and a কুইজ যোদ্ধা badge.

Count: mcq_user_quiz_daily.quiz_completed, cross-checked with user_activity_duration_agg.quiz_count have
Monthly curve: quizzes aggregated per month (12 points).
Check: SC-1737 flags the two quiz sources may not match — reconcile which is authoritative.
ভিডিও দেখা
Slide 5 · video

ভিডিও দেখা — ২,৮৬০ মিনিট

Minutes of video watched, told as film strips: প্রায় ২৪টা সিনেমার সমান (each cell = one 2-hour movie). Badge: ভিডিও ম্যারাথনার.

Data: recorded_videos_duration + animated_videos_duration (seconds); counts recorded_videos_count + animated_videos_count have
Framing: “movies” = total_minutes ÷ 120.
Tap → stamp falls
নিয়মিত পড়ুয়া · ১৮৭ দিন
Slide 6 · learner type (the signature moment)

তোমার পড়ার ধরন → নিয়মিত পড়ুয়া

This is the “learned something about myself” beat the CEO asked for. The student taps to drop a rubber stamp, and the year’s habit is named: নিয়মিত পড়ুয়া (Regular Studier). Under it: তুমি ১৮৭ দিন পড়তে এসেছ — সপ্তাহে গড়ে প্রায় ৫ দিন, on a GitHub-style activity grid, with the longest streak called out: ২ থেকে ২৪ আগস্ট, টানা ২৩ দিন!

Active days (১৮৭): distinct user_activity_duration_agg.activity_date — plus, per SC-1737, days where the only activity was Shikho AI (gen_ai_conversations), a QR scan (qr_scan_logs) or an eBook open GET-source
Streak (২৩): longest run of back-to-back active dates, with start/end.
Grid: the full list of active dates (one cell per day).
The stamp itself: the learner “type” — here the Rhythm axis (≥ ~5 active days/week → নিয়মিত). This is the one page that needs a type-assignment rule defined and, critically, a BI check that the types actually split across students define + verify

Open gaps — close before committing the date

1 · Best subject can’t be read directly. user_activity_duration_agg has no subject column; the “বছরের বিষয়” marksheet row must be rebuilt from three subject-carrying logs, and SC-1737 says it’s “not checked yet” whether they sum to the agg total. This gates the marksheet.
2 · The learner-type stamp needs a rule AND a distribution check. The stamp is the signature beat, but nothing defines how the label is assigned, and no one has confirmed the types actually split across 20–30k students. If most students get the same stamp, the moment dies. Run the BI distribution before writing copy.
3 · AI / QR / eBook “active days” are GET-sources. They live in service DBs, not datafuse. The ১৮৭-days count and the streak depend on them. Confirm they can be piped by ~10 Dec, or the active-day number is video/quiz/class-only.
4 · Favourite teacher is designed-out. Data exists; no slide in B. Product decision: add it or drop it.
