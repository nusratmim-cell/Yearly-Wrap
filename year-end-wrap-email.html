IN 30 SECONDS
What: A tap-through story showing each student their 2026: study days, hours, live classes, quizzes, videos, a learner personality, and a card to share.
Why: To celebrate their effort, bring them back before 2027, and spread the word about Shikho through shared cards.

3 VERSIONS

Which version works best?

Currently, we have thought of 3 versions of the same Year-End Wrap: A3 Poster, B3 Marksheet and C3 Journey. The story and data are the same, and only the look and feel change. Please share your feedback on these too, and tell us which one you think is better, and why.

THE STORY

What students will see

1Study activity
Total study days, learning hours and longest study streak, with a calendar of the whole year
2Live classes
Classes attended, with an achievement title such as "First Bencher"
3Quizzes and videos
Quizzes completed and time spent watching lessons, each with a title
4Learner personality
A fun, tap-to-reveal title based on their study habits, such as "Regular Learner"
5Shareable cards
Personalised cards for Facebook, Messenger and WhatsApp, with an option to hide their name

THE DATA

Where the numbers come from

Nothing new needs to be collected. Every number comes from activity Shikho already records when students use the app. At the end of the year, we'll add up each student's 2026 once and show it in their Wrap.

What students seeWhere it comes from
Name, class, groupThe student's profile
Study days, calendar, streakThe app's record of which days each student was active
Learning hours, top subjectThe time students spend in classes, videos and quizzes

Titles and personalityWorked out from the numbers above, using rules we'll agree on together
