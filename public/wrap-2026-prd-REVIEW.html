Strategy — the bet, the insight, the bar

The rest of this doc is the execution — screens, data, scope. This is the altitude: why this wins, the one risk that kills it, how big the prize is, and the single number we’re steering by. If we disagree here, nothing downstream matters.

The bet
Show every paid student a proud, personal, shareable story of their year → they come back in January, and their shared card sells Shikho to the exact peers we want next.

It’s the Spotify Wrapped playbook — identity, pride, social proof — moved from “your songs” to “your effort.” Two returns from one artifact: retention (a reason to re-engage when the new class starts) and acquisition (authentic, student-made proof shown to other students).

Why it works — the insight

Students systematically underweight their own effort. A year of work is invisible to them by December. Reflecting it back — 187 days, 142 hours, a 23-day streak — converts invisible effort into visible pride, and pride is the emotional fuel for returning. And a card carrying real numbers from a real student is the most credible ad we can put in front of their friends — who are precisely our target market, because students buy from students.

The learner-type stamp is the differentiator, not the stats. “I studied 142 hours” is a number; “I’m a রাতজাগা পড়ুয়া (Night Owl)” — or a পরিশ্রমী (Hard Worker) — is an identity, and identity is what people share. Statistics inform; identity travels. (Night Owl needs the hour-band in O8; if it can’t be sourced, the flagship example falls back to an always-computable axis — §8.6.)

The insight most teams miss — and the one rule everything obeys
We named it a “marksheet.” Marks imply judgment. Wrapped works because no one feels bad.

If a struggling student opens a marksheet and sees low marks or a poor rank, we manufacture shame and push away the exact students we’re trying to retain — the opposite of the goal. So the non-negotiable principle: every student must finish the wrap feeling proud.

This isn’t a slogan — it changes real decisions: lead with effort, not outcome; never show rank or “weakest subject” to low performers; Fresh Start for the quiet; tier copy that is always positively framed; hide-name ON by default on shares.

The highest-leverage point in the whole product

Every rupee of value is gated by one tap. Acquisition is downstream of share, share is downstream of finish, finish is downstream of open — and open is one banner on the home screen. Win the tap and everything is possible; lose it and the rest is zero. So we over-invest in the banner (personal number + launch push + first-open coachmark) and A/B the hook hardest.

The one number — and the guardrails

North Star
January-2027 return-lift: finishers vs comparable non-openers (within the qualified paid cohort)

Inputs we steer weekly: open → finish → share. We don’t optimise a vanity metric; we optimise whether reflected pride brings students back.

Clean read — the confound: opening is voluntary, so openers are already more engaged; a raw “finishers vs non-openers” gap overstates lift. We debias with a randomized hold-out — suppress the banner for a small random slice of the qualified cohort and compare intent-to-treat (got-banner vs held-out), not just openers vs non-openers.

Guardrail · no shame
Low-performer finish-rate ≥ overall finish-rate. If strugglers bail before the end, the experience is judging them — fix before scaling. This is our shame-sensor.
Guardrail · no harm
Zero rise in opt-outs / complaints; no privacy incident. A viral loop that leaks a minor’s data is a loss, not a win.
Guardrail · honest shares
Shared cards are accurate & privacy-safe by default (QA gate + name-hidden). We never buy reach with a wrong or over-exposing card.

What we are deliberately NOT doing — and why

iOS / web
Android-only v1. Narrow to the highest-intent, highest-value cohort and ship fast; widen once the bet is proven.
Friends / leaderboard compare inside the wrap
Direct peer-compare is the fastest route to shame — it violates the one rule. Deferred, maybe never.
In-app card editing · pre-2024 history
Scope and data cost without changing the core bet. V2 candidates.
Real-time compute
Precompute nightly — correctness, speed and cost all win; the wrap is a reflection, not a live dashboard.

What we’re really testing (so we learn, not just ship)

  • Does pride drive return? — the North Star A/B: finishers vs a held-out comparable non-opener group in January.
  • Does the hook drive the tap? — banner copy A (personal number) vs C (generic). The tap is the whole funnel’s gate.
  • Does identity drive sharing? — learner-type slide on/off; measure finish & share rate. If identity travels, doubling down on the stamp is the roadmap.
  • Are we shaming anyone? — low-performer finish-rate as a standing guardrail metric, watched from day one.

0.1 What we want to show — content checklist for the UI team

The Wrap turns a student’s year into a short, proud story. This is exactly what each student sees, and why. Full interactive reference: ▶ prototype ?v=b

  • Who they are — name · class · section — so it feels like theirs from screen one.
  • How much they studied — total hours shown as school days (e.g. ১৪২ ঘণ্টা → ২৪ স্কুল দিন), so the number means something.
  • How regular they were — active days + longest streak on a calendar grid — the backbone of the pride.
  • Live classes — joined vs held, with an attendance title (ফার্স্ট বেঞ্চার) — rewards showing up.
  • Quizzes — how many, rising through the year, with an “almost a champion” nudge.
  • Video — minutes watched shown as movies (e.g. ২,৮৬০ মিনিট → ২৪টা সিনেমা) — a scale they feel.
  • What kind of learner they were — one rubber-stamp personality (নিয়মিত পড়ুয়া…) — the “I learned something about myself” moment, different per student.
  • Their best subject — from quiz/exam accuracy — specific and personal.
  • A marksheet to share — all of it on one Shikho card, with a hide-name toggle — the viral payoff.
  • Returning students: years with Shikho (loyalty).  Low-activity students: a short, kind “Fresh Start” version — nobody feels bad.

1.0 What the Wrap is — in plain language

A paid student taps তোমার ২০২৬ খাতা on the home screen. It opens a full-screen, tap-through story — like Spotify Wrapped, styled as a Shikho notebook (খাতা) — that walks them through their whole year: how many hours they studied, how many live classes they attended, how many quizzes they did, how much video they watched, and finally what kind of learner they were, ending on a marksheet they can share. It is meant to feel personal and make them proud, so they come back in January.

2.0 Where the data comes from (provenance)

The wrap reads one warehouse — datafuse (Postgres) — but that warehouse is fed by the whole platform. Two paths fill it, and the wrap job sits on top of both; it never touches the source services directly, so it can’t slow the app, exams or live classes.

  • Live event stream. Each microservice (exam, live-exam, academic-program, live-class, gamification…) emits domain events over NATS JetStream / gRPC; the KPI service mirrors them into datafuse. (e.g. a model test publishes LESSON.ModelTest.<id>.VALIDATED; live-class attendance and exam results flow the same way.)
  • Nightly aggregation (Airflow, data_pipeline_bronze). DAGs roll raw activity into the aggregate tables the wrap uses — user_activity_duration_agg (7 activity-type durations), mcq_user_*_daily (quiz / question / subject), plus S3 bronze backups — every morning ~06:00–07:00 BD.
  • Operational stores not yet in the warehouse (MongoDB / ArangoDB): AI chats (gen-ai), model-test detail (live-exam), badges (gamification), QR scans. These are the GET-sources — §2.5 pipes them in.

2.2a Per-task computation — what each task actually runs

All queries run on datafuse over the window 2026-01-01 → 2026-12-20, scoped to the cohort from t1. Most of this assembles tables that already exist (see the source-of-truth map); verify the few column names marked ? with information_schema before coding.

-- t1 · cohort  (O1: eligibility = a paid enrolment that OVERLAPS the window, NOT payment-date-in-window)
-- ⚠ Do NOT filter on tran_date alone — that drops students who paid in 2025 for a 2026 course (§8.2 O1).
CREATE TEMP TABLE cohort AS
SELECT DISTINCT s.id AS user_id, t.course_id AS program_id
FROM "Students" s JOIN "Transactions" t ON t.student_id = s.id
WHERE t.status = 'paid'
  -- enrolment-active period overlaps 2026-01-01 .. 2026-12-20 (standard overlap test):
  AND COALESCE(t.enrolment_start, t.tran_date) <= '2026-12-20'
  AND COALESCE(t.enrolment_end, '9999-12-31') >= '2026-01-01';
-- confirm the enrolment-validity columns with the ST-25781 owner; fall back to course validity if absent.

-- t2 · engagement  (study time, by type, by month, video)
SELECT user_id,
  SUM(recorded_videos_duration + animated_videos_duration + live_class_duration
    + quiz_duration + live_exam_duration + model_test_duration + smart_note_duration) AS total_study_sec,
  SUM(recorded_videos_duration + animated_videos_duration) AS video_sec,
  SUM(recorded_videos_count + animated_videos_count)       AS video_count,
  SUM(quiz_count) AS quiz_count_agg
FROM user_activity_duration_agg
WHERE activity_date BETWEEN '2026-01-01' AND '2026-12-20'
GROUP BY user_id;
-- study_by_month[12]: add date_trunc('month',activity_date) to GROUP BY
-- study_by_type:     one SUM() per *_duration column

-- t3 · attendance + favourite teacher  (min-stay = 10 min OR 30% of class length)
-- held is scoped PER STUDENT to classes on/after THEIR enrolment date (§8.7/O6) — not all 2026 classes,
-- else mid-year enrollees get an inflated denominator → unfairly low rate (breaks the "no shame" rule).
WITH att AS (            -- aggregate rejoins BEFORE the min-stay test; MAX() de-fans co-taught classes
  SELECT sa.user_id, ta.content_id,
         SUM(sa.duration) AS stay, MAX(ta.duration) AS class_len
  FROM student_attendees sa
  JOIN teacher_attendees ta ON ta.content_id = COALESCE(NULLIF(sa.content_id,''), sa.room_id)
  GROUP BY sa.user_id, ta.content_id),
joined AS (
  SELECT user_id, COUNT(*) AS joined FROM att
  WHERE stay >= GREATEST(600, 0.30 * class_len) GROUP BY user_id),
held AS (
  SELECT c.user_id, COUNT(*) AS held
  FROM cohort c
  JOIN enrolment e ON e.user_id=c.user_id AND e.program_id=c.program_id
  JOIN lessons l ON l.academic_program_id = c.program_id AND l.content_type='LiveClass'
    AND l.start_time BETWEEN GREATEST(e.enrolment_start,'2026-01-01') AND '2026-12-20'
  GROUP BY c.user_id)
SELECT c.user_id, j.joined, h.held,
       LEAST(j.joined::numeric / NULLIF(h.held,0), 1) AS rate
FROM cohort c LEFT JOIN joined j USING(user_id) LEFT JOIN held h USING(user_id);
-- favourite teacher = most-attended (COUNT DISTINCT content so rejoins/co-teaching don't inflate):
SELECT DISTINCT ON (sa.user_id) sa.user_id, ta.teacher_id, COUNT(DISTINCT ta.content_id) n
FROM student_attendees sa
JOIN teacher_attendees ta ON ta.content_id = COALESCE(NULLIF(sa.content_id,''),sa.room_id)
GROUP BY sa.user_id, ta.teacher_id ORDER BY sa.user_id, n DESC;  -- join teacher_id → Teachers

-- t4 · quizzes
SELECT user_id, SUM(quiz_completed) AS quizzes FROM mcq_user_quiz_daily WHERE «day»? BETWEEN … GROUP BY user_id;
SELECT user_id, SUM(total_questions) q, SUM(total_correct) correct, SUM(hard_question_correct) hard
FROM mcq_user_chapter_daily WHERE «day»? BETWEEN … GROUP BY user_id;

-- t5 · active days + longest streak  (gaps-and-islands)
WITH d AS (SELECT DISTINCT user_id, activity_date FROM user_activity_duration_agg
         WHERE activity_date BETWEEN '2026-01-01' AND '2026-12-20'
         -- UNION distinct AI / QR days once piped (§2.5) ),
g AS (SELECT user_id, activity_date,
          activity_date - (ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY user_id ORDER BY activity_date))::int AS grp
        FROM d),
runs AS (SELECT user_id, grp, COUNT(*) len, MIN(activity_date) s, MAX(activity_date) e
           FROM g GROUP BY user_id, grp)
SELECT DISTINCT ON (user_id) user_id, len AS longest_streak, s AS streak_start, e AS streak_end
FROM runs ORDER BY user_id, len DESC;
-- active_days = COUNT(DISTINCT activity_date); avg_days_per_week = active_days / weeks_since_first_activity
-- GRAIN (O6): identity stats — active_days, longest_streak, active_dates — are computed PER USER (union of
-- all the student's programs) and written identically into each (user,program) row; only program-specific
-- stats (attendance, that program's quizzes) differ per row. The banner shows the PRIMARY program's row.

-- t6 · best / weakest subject  (subject-native accuracy — no time rebuild)
WITH subj AS (
  SELECT user_id, subject_name,
    SUM(total_correct)::numeric / NULLIF(SUM(total_questions),0) AS accuracy,
    SUM(total_questions) AS q
  FROM mcq_user_chapter_daily
  WHERE «day»? BETWEEN '2026-01-01' AND '2026-12-20'
    AND lower(subject_name) NOT IN ('orientation','orientation and guideline')
  GROUP BY user_id, subject_name)
SELECT DISTINCT ON (user_id) user_id, subject_name AS top_subject, accuracy
FROM subj WHERE q >= :min_questions          -- a setting, e.g. 20; weakest = ORDER BY accuracy ASC
ORDER BY user_id, accuracy DESC;

-- t7a · badges = the HIGHEST tier each metric clears (DISTINCT ON keeps one row per metric, not every tier)
SELECT DISTINCT ON (m.user_id, c.metric) m.user_id, c.badge_code, c.label_bn
FROM student_metrics m JOIN wrap_tier_config c ON
   (c.metric='attendance_rate' AND m.rate           >= c.min_value)
OR (c.metric='quiz_completed'  AND m.quizzes        >= c.min_value)
OR (c.metric='video_minutes'   AND m.video_sec/60   >= c.min_value)
OR (c.metric='longest_streak'  AND m.longest_streak >= c.min_value)
ORDER BY m.user_id, c.metric, c.min_value DESC;   -- highest min_value cleared wins per metric

t7b · Learner-type — cohort-relative, self-balancing (the signature stamp)

One stamp per student. It is chosen relative to the cohort, so the types spread ~evenly whatever the raw distribution looks like (why this removes the distribution risk — §7.1 R1):

  1. Per student compute each axis raw value: rhythm=active_days/weeks_enrolled · intensity=total_study_sec · clock=night_share (needs an hour-band column; drop this axis if absent) · method=active_sec/(active_sec+video_sec) · focus=top_subject_accuracy − mean_subject_accuracy.
  2. Per axis, compute the cohort mean μ and stddev σ; then z = (x − μ) / σ.
  3. The stamp = the axis with the largest |z| (the trait the student is most extreme on), mapped via wrap_type_rule (z>0 → the z_high label, z<0 → its z_low opposite).
  4. Fallback: if every |z| < 0.5 → the balanced stamp (ভারসাম্যপূর্ণ) — nobody gets a blank.
WITH base AS (   -- baseline EXCLUDES Fresh-Start (§8.6) so near-zero activity can't drag μ/σ
  SELECT * FROM student_axis_values WHERE NOT is_fresh_start),
z AS (SELECT user_id,
    (rhythm    - avg(rhythm)    OVER())/NULLIF(stddev_pop(rhythm)    OVER(),0) AS z_rhythm,
    (intensity - avg(intensity) OVER())/NULLIF(stddev_pop(intensity) OVER(),0) AS z_intensity,
    (method    - avg(method)    OVER())/NULLIF(stddev_pop(method)    OVER(),0) AS z_method,
    (focus     - avg(focus)     OVER())/NULLIF(stddev_pop(focus)     OVER(),0) AS z_focus
    -- + z_clock only when O8 lands an hour-band; + z_accuracy/z_pace/z_attendance once their baselines pass (§8.6)
  FROM base)
-- then, per user, pick the axis with MAX(abs(z)) and map (axis, sign) → wrap_type_rule.label_bn
-- Fresh-Start students are NOT stamped here (they get the 2-slide deck); |z|<0.5 for all → 'balanced'.
-- sanity-check: the chosen stamp should spread across students, not collapse to one.

2.6 Reliability, idempotency & observability

The existing pipeline is at-most-once with no retries, no DLQ and no alerting (a documented limitation of the CRM-sync / aggregation DAGs — silent gaps). The wrap is launch-critical and share-facing, so it must be stricter:

ConcernRequirement
IdempotencyUPSERT on (user_id, program_id) — never append (do not inherit the MCQ-agg append-mode dup bug). Reruns are safe.
RetriesAirflow retries ≥ 2 with backoff (existing DAGs use none) — a transient Mongo/PG blip must not drop a student.
QA gate (t10)Row stays is_available=false until it passes: no NULL headline stats, study ≤ 24h/day, rate ≤ 1, streak ≤ active_days, no negatives. Bad rows are quarantined + counted, never shipped.
FreshnessRuns after the upstream aggregations finish (~07:30 BD); a completeness check confirms every source table has the day before building. Final locked run: night of 20 Dec.
ObservabilityPer-run metrics: cohort size, rows built, rows quarantined (by reason), % populating each slide. Alert if quarantine-rate spikes or cohort drops vs the prior run.

8.1Architecture at a glance

Four layers, one guarantee each. The whole wrap is precomputed — the app never does live maths, and the source services (exams, live classes, payments) are never touched on a tap.

7.1 Key design choices (these remove the three unknowns)

Three choices make the design correct under every outcome, so the open numbers only need tuning, never a go/no-go.

R1 · Learner-type distribution → cohort-relative assignment, even by construction.
  • Design: per student, z-score each axis (rhythm = active-days/week · clock = night share · method = active-vs-video · focus = top-subject share · intensity = total study time). Stamp = the axis where the student is furthest from the cohort median. Because it’s relative, stamps spread ~evenly no matter how skewed any single raw metric is.
  • Branches: even spread → ship · one axis still dominates → drop it from the arg-max set (config) · all flat → fall back to a 2-tier intensity stamp. All three ship on time.
R2 · Best subject → subject-native accuracy, not time reconstruction.
  • Design: “বছরের বিষয় / strongest subject” = highest accuracy in mcq_user_chapter_daily grouped by subject_name (filter “orientation”, require ≥ min questions) — a real subject column, no 3-log rebuild, no sum reconciliation. Exactly SC-1737’s “Subjects and chapters” method.
  • Time-by-subject (the fragile rebuild) is demoted to an optional slide, off the marksheet’s critical path.
  • Fallback: a student with too few graded questions for a reliable top subject → use “most-practised subject” (question count), still subject-native.
R3 · AI/QR active days → additive, never load-bearing.
  • Design: active_days ships from the warehouse set alone; AI/QR/eBook days are a bonus union that only increases the number if piped (the spec’s own intent). Copy is true either way.
  • Branch: not piped by 10 Dec → number is class/quiz/video-based; feature unaffected.

Net: after R1–R3, the only thing left to settle is the numbers (which axis dominates, the min-questions cut) — never whether we ship.
