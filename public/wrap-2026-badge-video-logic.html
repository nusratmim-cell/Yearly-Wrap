Badge & Eligibility — Business Logic

Business rules for Wrap 2026 badges and eligibility: who gets a wrap, how each badge is earned (with corner cases), who sees what, and how after-launch changes are handled.

2026-11-04 · Companion to the Wrap PRD. Supersedes the earlier plan to read badges from the gamification service (now discontinued).

1Who gets a wrap — everyone

Every student account gets the full wrap, paid or not — no paid gate, no qualifying window. The wrap uses whatever activity each student has; busy students see fuller sections, quiet ones see encouraging copy (Section 3).

Account typeGets the full wrap?Note
Paid — active, expired, lapsed, or refundedYesThe richest wraps; refund/expiry does not remove it
TrialYesUses whatever they did; thin sections show encouraging copy
Free (never paid)YesSame template; a reason to return and a card to share
Staff / teacher / test / internalNoNot students — excluded, and kept out of all peer comparisons
Deleted / bannedNoNo account to show a wrap for
Account created after 20 Dec (during/after launch week)No 2026 wrapNo activity in the 2026 window to reflect; they start fresh for 2027 — no banner until they have window activity

Duplicate accounts for one student merge to a single wrap (else the most-active account gets it). ⚠ This reverses the PRD’s locked “paid-only” + “Fresh Start” decisions — the PRD’s eligibility section (Section 8.2 / state matrix) must be updated to match.

2Definitions — every term used here

The window
1 Jan – 20 Dec 2026, counted in Bangladesh time. Only activity inside it counts; activity after 20 Dec is not included this year.
Active day
A calendar day with any real learning activity — video, live class, quiz, notes, or Shikho AI. Merely opening the app does not count. Counted once per day, in Bangladesh time.
Quiz completed
A quiz the student finished/submitted — not opened-and-abandoned. Counted per distinct quiz (retakes don’t multiply).
Questions (for accuracy)
Distinct MCQ questions the student answered in the window (excluding orientation), counting the first attempt at each.
Video watch time
Content actually watched — recorded + animated combined — measured by content position reached (so 1.5×/2× speed isn’t penalised), counted once per video up to its length (rewatching doesn’t inflate).
Live attendance
Joining a class live and staying ≥ 10 minutes or 30% of the class. Watching the recording later is video, not attendance.
Video pool
All students with 30+ minutes of video, all grades nationally. The ranking pool for the video badge, so near-zero students don’t distort it.
Years with Shikho
Distinct calendar years with an active paid enrolment, from the earliest reliable record. Records exist from 2024 (max 3 this year). Trial/free = 0 → no loyalty badge.
Primary course
For a multi-course student, the one they were most active in (tie → most recent). The banner opens this one; attendance is measured here.

3One wrap for all — thin data handled gracefully

One wrap template, everyone gets it — no shortened version. The difference between a busy and a quiet student is handled inside the same wrap, section by section: solid data → the full visual; a little data (below a section’s show-floor) → a short encouraging line, never an empty chart; no data → the section is skipped. We never show “0” or a blank grid; we show “--” or warmth.

No student ever sees “nothing.” There is a guaranteed floor every wrap always shows, no matter how little the student did: the cover with their name, a warm personal message, their learner-type stamp (always assigned — Section 7), a welcome marker (loyalty if returning, else “নতুন শুরু”), and an invitation to build their 2027 — ending on a shareable marksheet. A quiet student simply sees fewer data sections and more encouragement; they never reach a blank screen.

So a student with little video (or little of anything) doesn’t lose the wrap — they lose only that section’s chart, which is replaced by an encouraging line, while the guaranteed floor above always carries the experience.

4Where badges come from

The old badge service is dead. We had planned to read badges from the gamification service; review confirmed it (and the Textpack reference) has been unused ~3 years, with no list of what’s still live. We rely on none of it. Every badge is calculated from the student’s own recorded activity. If a badge’s source data is unreliable for a given student, that badge simply isn’t shown for them (no error); if a whole source is down, that badge is withdrawn for everyone (never partially awarded) and the rest still ship.

5The badge roster

Eleven badges, across everything the wrap already measures — so there are many ways to be recognised and almost every active student earns at least one. Each is earned purely from the student’s own activity in the window, at its highest reached tier only. Earning none is still fine: the badge area hides and nothing reads as failure.

The four “bases” — how each bar is set. Relative: raw time is unbounded and drifts year to year, so we rank against peers (study time, video). Milestone: a round, chase-able number, sanity-checked against the real spread (quizzes, questions, active days, streak, loyalty years). Rate: already a percentage, so an absolute cut is meaningful (accuracy, attendance). Personal: measured against the student’s own year or subjects, never peers (improvement, best subject). Thresholds live in config and retune with no app release. Final Bangla names are a brand pass — the rules are what’s fixed here.

7The learner-type stamp — one personality per student

Separate from badges. The stamp is the signature “this is the kind of learner I was” moment — exactly one per student, always present (badges are 0–6; the stamp is always 1). It has its own slide; badges have the collection.

StampGiven to the student who most stands out on…
নিয়মিত পড়ুয়া — ConsistentStudying across the most days/weeks (regularity)
পরিশ্রমী — Hard workerThe most total study time (effort)
অনুশীলনকারী — PracticerLearning most by doing quizzes/practice vs watching
রাতজাগা পড়ুয়া — Night owlThe largest share of study at night (needs time-of-day data — see note)
একনিষ্ঠ / অনুসন্ধানী — Focused / ExplorerConcentrating on one subject · or spreading across many
ভারসাম্যপূর্ণ — BalancedNo single trait stands out — the default, never a blank
How the stamp is chosen. We look at each study dimension and give the student the stamp for the one where they stand out most compared to other students — not a fixed cut-off. Because it’s relative, the stamps spread naturally across students (not everyone becomes the same type), whatever the raw numbers look like. If nothing stands out → ভারসাম্যপূর্ণ.

Corner cases

  • Always one, never empty — every student gets a stamp.
  • Thin data: a dimension we can’t measure for a student (e.g. no graded questions) is skipped for them — they still get a stamp from the rest.
  • Just-starting students (almost no activity) get a gentle welcome stamp rather than a comparison-based one.
  • If two dimensions tie for most distinctive, a fixed priority order breaks the tie, so reruns are stable.
  • Night owl depends on time-of-day data. If it isn’t reliably available this year, that one personality isn’t offered and the rest still work — but it’s the flagship example in Strategy, so securing it is a priority. data check

8Who sees which badges — the no-shame display

Same badge engine, emphasised differently by how the year went. Classification is computable, and the groups are checked top-down (Strong first) and are mutually exclusive — so earning any badge lifts a student out of “Just starting”:

GroupHow a student lands hereLead withNever show
StrongEarned 3+ badges, or top 20% of the video pool by activityAll earned badges · a positive “top X%” · best subject— (let them flex)
MiddleEarned 1–2 badges (not Strong)Earned effort badges · hours · active daysRank · peer comparison
Low0 badges but some real activityEffort stats + optional near-miss nudgeRank · “weakest” anything · empty shelf
Just startingAlmost no activityThe warm welcome wrap + welcome/loyalty markerCharts · totals · any comparison

Standing is only ever a positive “top X%,” shown only to Strong students — never a numeric class rank, never to the others.

9Cross-cutting fairness guards

The guards that apply across all badges (the per-badge ones live in Section 5).

GuardRule
Window & timezoneOnly activity in 1 Jan – 20 Dec 2026, Bangladesh time counts toward any badge.
Non-students excluded from poolsStaff/test/internal accounts are removed before the video percentile and stamp comparisons, so they can’t skew the cuts.
No double countingRepeated opens / rejoins of the same class or video are counted once.
Per-student missing dataIf one badge’s source is unreliable for a student, that badge simply isn’t shown for them — never an error, never a guess.
Stable & finalBadges are locked on the final data run; they don’t change under the student after launch.

10After launch — events

These are business rules, not implementation: they decide whether a student keeps a badge/card after something changes.

EventWhat happens
Student refunds / lets a course expire after launchNo change — the wrap isn’t payment-gated, so they keep it and their badges.
Account deleted after sharingThe public card is revoked — the shared link stops working.
A threshold/number is changed after some cards existThe next data run recomputes everyone; cards already captured/shared are pinned to what they showed.
A data source breaks before the final runThat badge is withdrawn for everyone (never partial); the rest still ship; the wrap is never blocked.

11Shared card & reaching non-users

  • Every student can share their card. Badges are safe to show publicly (positive, no private data); the card still defaults to name hidden (many are minors).
  • A true non-user (no Shikho account) who opens a shared card sees the public marksheet + a clear “তুমিও তোমার খাতা বানাও — Shikho-তে শুরু করো” sign-up prompt — the acquisition edge.
