2.5 Platform-wide gotchas verified

  • No foreign keys declared anywhere — relations are by id/code columns only.
  • Soft delete: is_active=false; academic-program tables also is_deleted=true.
  • Server timestamps are BD-local (UTC+6) when payload omits them (utility/date.go) — time-of-day math must account for this.
  • Warehouse DBs: shikho_dev (default), shikho_ucc; sslmode=require. Aggregates land in schema datafuse.
