00Read this first: what Wrapped actually is

Most people think Spotify Wrapped is "a summary of your year in music." That's the surface. If we build from that belief, we'll ship a prettier stats screen and miss — which is exactly the trap the CEO called out.

The designs look initially nice but we are mostly representing user data attractively. Let's go a step deeper… Spotify Wrapped generates insights based on the individual… My experience would be — wow, I learned something new about myself. — Ishmam Ahmed Chowdhury, CEO

Here's the real definition the team should hold: Wrapped is an identity product with a growth engine attached. It takes raw behaviour, derives something that feels like a truth about you, gives you a word for it, and hands you a card built to be shared. The stats are just the raw material. Spotify has spent four years moving away from "here are your numbers" and toward "here is who you are." That direction of travel is the whole lesson.

Our one-line north star: a student should finish the Shikho Wrap and think "that's so me" — then send it to a friend to prove it. Every decision below serves that sentence.

01The 7 principles — the lens we'll reuse all doc

Before the year-by-year teardown, here's the framework underneath all of it. When you look at any Wrapped slide, ask which of these it's doing. We'll tag the teardown against them.

1
Stats are the floor; identity is the ceiling. Nobody screenshots "142 hours." They screenshot "I'm a Night-Owl Specialist." The numbers earn trust; the label gets shared. Build up from stats toward identity.
2
Derive, don't display. Every memorable feature — Aura, Listening Personality, Sound Town — is computed, not shown. The magic is in the function that turns 5 numbers into 1 meaning.
3
Variable slides create uniqueness. Your Wrapped ≠ your friend's Wrapped because slides are gated by eligibility. The engine picks a subset. This is the single biggest thing our fixed-slide prototype is missing.
4
Give people a word for themselves. An archetype ("The Adventurer", "Shapeshifter") is a gift: it's identity, it's language, it's inherently social. Humans love a category that flatters.
5
Engineer one "how did it know that?" moment. Cross two signals into a revelation the user never stated. This is the CEO's "learned something about myself." It's usually a combination, not a single metric.
6
Design the share, not just the screen. The share card is the growth loop. For a brand chasing 20–30k students' friends, the card is the cheapest acquisition channel we have.
7
Close with forward motion. Spotify ends on playlists you'll keep playing. We must end by pulling the student back in January — that's literally our success metric (return rate). Celebration → next step.

022021  The year Wrapped stopped being a list

Until 2021, Wrapped was mostly leaderboards: top songs, artists, genres, minutes. In 2021 Spotify added interpretation and play. This is where the template we're copying was born.

Core stats carried over top artists · top songs · top genres · minutes listened · top podcasts
Why
The floor (Principle 1). Trust + "oh yeah, that's true." Necessary, not memorable.
Shikho
Core cards: active days, total hours, top subject, favourite teacher, quizzes done, most-consumed content. HAVE — all in the warehouse.
Audio Aura signature 2021
What
Your top two moods rendered as a two-colour aura (built with an aura reader for flavour). A vibe, not a number.
Why
Principle 2 + 6. It converts abstract data into a sharable visual identity. Nothing to read — pure screenshot bait.
Shikho
"স্টাডি অরা" — two dominant signals (e.g. top subject + study-timing) → a two-tone aura/gradient per student. Cheap to compute, maximally shareable. BUILD (visual rule over data we have).
Playing Cards — "2 Truths & a Lie" signature 2021
What
A mini-game: three "facts" about your listening, one is false — you guess.
Why
Principle 5, as interaction. Guessing makes you study your own data. Dwell time + delight, almost free to build.
Shikho
"দুটো সত্যি, একটা মিথ্যা" — pull 3 real facts from the student's year + generate 1 plausible-but-false one; they tap to guess. Perfect for a student audience. BUILD.
2021: The Movie  ·  Wrapped Blend
What
"The Movie" narrated your year as film scenes. "Blend" compared your taste with a friend's.
Shikho
Narrative framing (your year as acts: slow start → exam-season climax) and a "স্টাডি ব্লেন্ড" friend-compare via the share link. Both are BUILD, and Blend is a growth loop — tag for v2.

Takeaway from 2021: the leap from "list" to "interpretation + play" is the same leap we need to make from our current fixed-slide prototype.

032022  The identity engine — the year we're copying most

If you read only one section, read this. 2022 is where Wrapped became a personality product, and it maps almost one-to-one onto Shikho.

Listening Personality signature 2022 · THE one to copy
What
16 personality types (The Replayer, The Specialist, The Adventurer, The Fanclubber…), each a 4-letter code from four binary axes: familiarity↔exploration, loyalty↔variety, timeliness↔newness, commonality↔uniqueness. Deliberately Myers-Briggs-flavoured.
Why
Principles 1→4 in one slide. Four simple axes → a 4-bit code → 16 identities that feel scientific and flattering and ownable. "I'm an ADVE" is a flex. This is the engine, not a gimmick.
Shikho
"শিখো Learner Personality" — our direct equivalent. Use 4 binary axes we can measure: Consistency (sporadic↔regular), Timing (early↔night), Mode (video↔quiz/live), Depth (all-rounder↔specialist) → a 4-bit code → 16 named Bangla types. The finale card. BUILD; all four axes come from data we HAVE.
Design note for the team: don't invent 16 vague labels first. Define the 4 axes first (each a clean threshold on real data), and the 16 types fall out of the combinations. That's how Spotify kept it rigorous — and how we keep it tunable as settings.
Audio Day signature 2022
What
How your listening (and its moods) shifts across morning / midday / evening.
Why
Principle 5. A time-of-day cut feels intimate — it's about your habits, not your taste.
Shikho
"তোমার পড়ার দিন" — morning/afternoon/night/after-midnight study split, and which subject you study in each band ("Physics at night, Math in the morning"). We added subject×time-of-day to the spec for exactly this. BUILD over user_activity_agg + subject logs.

042023  Characters & belonging

2023 doubled down on identity and added belonging — tying the individual to something bigger.

Me in 2023 — 12 characters signature 2023
What
Each listener assigned one of 12 "characters" (Shapeshifter, Luminary, Alchemist, Hypnotist, Hunter…), revealed by flipping a card.
Why
Principle 4 again — but note the flip-to-reveal interaction: anticipation before payoff. The reveal is as important as the label.
Shikho
Reuse the reveal mechanic for our personality finale — our C3 prototype already ends on a door that opens. Keep personality as one system (don't ship two overlapping archetype features). BUILD (interaction).
Sound Town signature 2023
What
Matches you to a real city whose listeners share your taste (e.g. a Dave fan → London). Belonging + a surprising external anchor.
Why
Principle 5 at its most magical — "a whole town listens like me?" It externalises identity into something relatable and shareable.
Shikho
"তোমার স্টাডি টুইন" — match the student to an archetype cohort they resemble (a study-pattern "town"/tribe), or the month/season they peaked with. Ambitious — needs a clustering model and careful, truthful framing (no fake rankings). STRETCH; park for v2 unless we can back it honestly.
Top-5 artists with peak month  ·  genre graphic
Shikho
Top subjects, each with the month it peaked, and a subject-mix visual. Directly supported by our per-month, per-subject splits. HAVE / light BUILD.

052024  AI — and the warning that comes with it

2024 pushed into AI and "evolution." Some of it is genuinely for us; some of it is a cautionary tale. Both matter.

Your Music Evolution signature 2024 · strong for us
What
Splits your year into 3 "phases" with quirky names and playlists (e.g. "Pumpkin Spice Hollywood Indie").
Why
Principle 5 over time. A year has a shape; naming its chapters makes it a story, not a stat.
Shikho
"তোমার ২০২৬ এর অধ্যায়" — cluster the year into phases from monthly data: e.g. "slow start", "the grind", "exam sprint", each with its dominant subject & behaviour. Rich, personal, and we have the monthly series. BUILD.
Wrapped AI Podcast signature 2024 · stretch
What
An AI-hosted audio episode (built on Google's NotebookLM) narrating your year. Time-limited, English-only, a handful of countries.
Why
Meets users in a new format — and is very on-brand for us: we already run Gen-AI, transcription and Bangla LLM pipelines (gen-ai, anubadok, transcriptor).
Shikho
"তোমার ২০২৬, অডিওতে" — an AI Bangla voice recap of the student's year. Genuinely differentiating, but v2: substance first. STRETCH (Gen-AI + TTS).
AI Playlist  ·  Longest Streak
Shikho
AI Playlist → the retention play: "২০২৭ স্টাডি প্ল্যান" — generate a personalised next-step plan from their 2026 data. This is Principle 7 made concrete: celebration → come back in January. BUILD (Gen-AI). Longest streak we already HAVE.
The 2024 warning (important): 2024 Wrapped got real backlash — fans felt it leaned on AI novelty while cutting the substantive stats (no "top genres" the usual way, thinner data). Lesson for us: AI is garnish, not the meal. Earn the "wow" with real derived insight first; add AI formats only once the core is strong. If we ship an AI podcast over a hollow core, we'll get the same reaction — from a student base that's far less forgiving of a flex that doesn't deliver.

07Our five signature features for v1

Spotify is remembered for 2–3 signature slides a year, not 20. Here's the focused set I'd commit to — enough to feel like Wrapped, few enough to ship well, every one backed by data we have.

1 · শিখো Learner Personality

The finale. 4 measurable axes → 16 Bangla types. This is the feature people will share. (≈ Listening Personality)

2 · তোমার পড়ার দিন

Subject × time-of-day — the intimate "you study Physics at night" revelation. (≈ Audio Day)

3 · তোমার ২০২৬ এর অধ্যায়

Your year in named phases, each with its subject & mood. The story spine. (≈ Music Evolution)

4 · দুটো সত্যি, একটা মিথ্যা

The interactive game that makes them study their own year. Cheap, delightful, sticky. (≈ Playing Cards)

5 · ২০২৭ স্টাডি প্ল্যান

The close that pulls them back in January — our actual success metric. (≈ AI Playlist)

Plus the core stat cards and the two CEO asks (favourite teacher, best exam subject) as the floor. Everything else (Aura, Study Twin, AI podcast, Blend) is a v2 backlog — real, but not what we prove the concept on.

08What NOT to copy

  • Don't lead with AI. The 2024 backlash is the proof. Substance first; AI formats are a v2 garnish.
  • Don't fabricate comparisons. "Top 5% in your class" is fine (we compute it); a made-up "study twin city" is not. Truthful or cut.
  • Don't ship 20 slides. Wrapped feels tight because it's curated. Our engine's job is to cut to 9–12 per student, not to show everything.
  • Don't ignore the low-data student. Spotify's users all listen a lot; many of our 30k won't have rich data. The gate + gentle fallback matters more for us than it ever did for Spotify — a hollow wrap for a light user is worse than none.
  • Don't forget the phone. Cheap Android, patchy data. The web-story + light assets approach (C3) is a feature, not a compromise.
  • Don't copy the vibe, copy the method. Bangla copy, exam-student psychology, teacher respect — the tone is ours; only the engine is Spotify's.
